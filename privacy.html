Privacy policy

Effective: 29 October 2026

Tidemark ("the app") is built so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Summary

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.
  • Your watchlist, portfolios, paper accounts and settings are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your watchlist, the trades you record, paper-trading accounts, followed strategies and settings in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data. A backup code is created on the device and is only shared if you choose to share it.

Market data requests

To show prices, the app requests public data directly from these services: CoinGecko, alternative.me, Upbit, Bithumb, Coinone, Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase, Kraken, bitFlyer, Frankfurter (ECB), open.er-api.com, TradingView, mempool.space, Blockscout. Like any internet request, these services receive your IP address and the data requested (for example, which coin's price). They do not receive your watchlist or portfolio. Their own privacy policies apply. The chart on the coin page is an embedded TradingView widget, which may use cookies under TradingView's policy.

Wallet address (optional)

When you create a paper account you may paste a public Bitcoin or Ethereum address to use its balance as virtual starting money. The address is sent to mempool.space or Blockscout to read the balance, and only a shortened form is kept on your device. The app never asks for or accepts seed phrases or private keys and cannot move funds.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, strategy alerts are created on your device. No push-notification server is used.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
