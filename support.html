Help & contact

Does Tidemark trade with real money?

No. Tidemark never places orders, holds funds or connects to an exchange account. Paper trading and strategy automation use virtual money only. The "Trade" button on a coin page simply opens the exchange's own website or app.

Where do the prices come from?

Rankings and market caps from CoinGecko; prices from the public APIs of Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase, Kraken, Upbit, Bithumb, Coinone and bitFlyer; exchange rates from Coinbase and the European Central Bank. Prices refresh every few seconds and can be slightly delayed.

What is the kimchi premium?

How much higher (or lower) a coin trades on Korean exchanges than on global exchanges, after converting at the live USD/KRW rate. You choose which exchanges are compared in Settings.

Why does a price differ from my exchange?

The global price is an average of the exchanges you select in Settings > Price source exchanges. Pick a single exchange to match it exactly.

How do strategy alerts work?

Follow a strategy in Portfolio > Strategies and allow notifications. iOS checks in the background from time to time, so alerts can arrive a little late. Backtests use past prices and do not predict future results.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Settings > Backup > Back up creates a code. On the new phone, open Settings > Backup > Restore and paste it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
